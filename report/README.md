📊 Key Insights from Churn Analysis
Customers with Month-to-month contracts show the highest churn rate, meaning they leave faster than long-term customers.
Fiber optic internet users have higher churn, which may indicate service quality or pricing issues.
New customers (low tenure) are more likely to leave compared to long-term customers.
Overall churn is driven mainly by short-term and flexible contract users.

💡 Business Recommendations
Encourage customers to choose long-term plans using discounts or offers.
Improve Fiber optic service quality or review pricing.
Focus on onboarding experience to retain new customers in early months.
Introduce loyalty programs for long-term users.
